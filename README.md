# 🤠 Lab Rodeo

Funny cowboy-themed flash cards and quiz for learning blood and urine lab markers, vitamins, hormones and common supplements.

**Play it:** https://wolftronixsd.github.io/lab-rodeo/

## What's inside

- **79 cards**: blood, urine, vitamins & minerals, hormones and supplements. Each card covers what the marker is, the typical range, what high or low means, and how to change it.
- **Quiz**: about 158 questions, score and streak tracking, and missed questions come back until you get them right.
- **Answers list**: every question with its answer, sorted A–Z, by section, by body part, or by US vs Europe.
- **Learning helpers**: "What is it?" hints, memory tricks (with a 🎵 Sing it button), word roots and abbreviations, and a fun icon per card.
- **Europe vs US**: European units and limits (EFSA, ESC/EAS, KDIGO, WHO, EAU) converted back to US units, color-coded same or different.
- **Hands-free**: auto-play for flash cards and the quiz (countdown, wrong answers crossed out, then the right one).
- Swipe left/right, haptics, sounds, light and dark mode.

It's a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step.

## Sources

MedlinePlus and the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (US); EFSA, ESC/EAS, KDIGO, WHO and EAU (Europe). Each card links to its source.

**Not medical advice.** Ranges are typical adult values and differ by lab, age, sex and method. Talk to a doctor before changing meds or starting supplements.
